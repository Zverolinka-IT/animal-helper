## Account management

Invitations create ordinary backoffice operators. They do not inherit account
management permission. Previously that permission could only be granted with
`pnpm admin:bootstrap -- --email person@example.com --grant-management` against
the intended environment.

After deployment, the account-management screen lists enrolled accounts and can
grant/revoke this permission after fresh passkey confirmation. An existing
manager changes another account; self-demotion is prohibited to avoid losing the
last manager. Lenka's production permissions are not changed by editing code.
